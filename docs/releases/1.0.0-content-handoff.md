## Claims to keep precise

- Announce a GitHub **source release**. Do not imply this release publishes an App Store/Google Play listing, installer binary, container image or hosted SaaS.
- Jev is optional, advisory and separately configured. It does not authorize changes, guarantee correctness or replace human approval. Hosted model use can cost money; do not claim the entire stack operates offline or for free.
- Do not call all 233 skills/173 integrations “live tested,” “always connected” or “production certified.” Selection, credentials and reachable infrastructure determine availability.
- Offline tests and builds passed; live gateway/RAG/provider/browser/phone/watch/per-host acceptance still has documented gaps. The broader flagship audit is not declared complete.
- Network changes retain baseline, ITSM approval, verification and audit expectations. Avoid “unrestricted autonomous network control” claims.
- The HUD shows configured inventory and available evidence; empty/unavailable/stale states are explicit. Do not describe estimated token usage as a guaranteed invoice or GCF estimates as guaranteed cost savings.
- No invented adoption numbers, performance figures, customer endorsements or demos. Label hypothetical workflows as examples and screenshots of synthetic data as demonstrations.
- Credit **Adam Mason** for Canvas work and **Sean Mahoney** for the community guide when discussing them; do not invent endorsement or quotes.
